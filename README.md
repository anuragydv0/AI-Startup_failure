## 🚀 Overview

**DEADPOOL AI** is a cinematic AI-powered platform that analyzes startup pitch decks and predicts the probability of startup failure using advanced AI reasoning, startup pattern analysis, and failure comparison intelligence.

Upload your pitch deck.
Our AI analyzes it like a ruthless VC who has reviewed 10,000+ startups and watched most of them die.

Instead of fake positivity, DEADPOOL AI gives:

* Failure probability score
* Brutally honest reasoning
* Weakness breakdown
* Failed startup comparisons
* Actionable survival recommendations
* Viral “Roast Mode”

---

### 📂 Pitch Deck Upload

* Upload PDF or PPTX pitch decks

  * Financials
